How the three months run

Month one · Map You show us the messy reality. An hour a week. You walk us through a renewal from expiring schedule to sent email, we name the steps that eat the time, and we set Tenzi up around how you actually work. The more honest, the better the result.
Month two · Use Real renewals go through it. Your next batch of pre-renewals, drafted by Tenzi, checked by you, sent from your own Outlook. A short check-in each week: what's saving time, what's annoying, what's missing. We fix what you feel, not what we guess.
Month three · Measure We count the hours together. One deeper session. Hours back per week against what month one measured, translated into dollars you can defend. Then we agree what ongoing access costs. You decide, on evidence, whether to keep going.

Why I'm building thisRoshan Khozouei

I came to this as a buyer.

Getting business insurance for my own company took months of back and forth, and I came out curious about the other side of the process. So I started asking brokers, more than fifty of them this year, how the work actually gets done. What I found was people who had gone out on their own, doing skilled work for clients, and losing their evenings to admin nobody pays for. I run a small business too. This is built for that.

The lesson from a decade in AI is that the hard part was never the technology. It was fitting it to how people actually work. That is what the three months are for.

The product

What does Tenzi actually do today?

It builds your pre-renewal emails. Tenzi reads the expiring policy, drafts the email in your words with a client-ready summary, and you send it from your own Outlook once you've looked it over. New business form filling is next. Claims is in design with partners. We'd rather tell you what works now than list what's coming.

Who it's for

Who is it for?

Australian general insurance brokers who run renewals for real clients: Authorised Representatives under a licensee, and brokerages holding their own AFSL. If the renewal pile is what stops you growing the book, you're who we build for.

Cost

Licensees

I'm under a licensee. Can I still join?

Yes, with their yes. Licensees rightly want to know where client data goes and who can see it. We bring a short governance summary and a walkthrough for them. Brokers with their own AFSL skip this step.

Your data

Where does client data go?

Tenzi runs on Microsoft Azure in the Asia-Pacific region and connects to your own Microsoft 365 mailbox, with your admin's consent. Nothing goes to a client or an insurer without your sign-off, and you can see where each detail in a draft came from.
